About · Privacy

Nothing leaves this device.

Strip runs in your browser. Files are read, split and encoded here, and decoded by the camera on the other device.

Privacy No account What it does not do Limits Credits

Privacy

No upload, no server copy, no share link. Your file is processed by the page you have open. The receiving phone reads light with its camera and rebuilds the file locally. Video from the camera is analysed on the device and is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Once the page has loaded, Strip does not need a connection to send or receive.

No account

No sign-up, no login, no profile. Close the tab and the session is gone.

What it does not do

Strip does not encrypt

Anyone who can see or photograph a strip, or the screen while a code plays, can rebuild the file. Treat a printed strip like the file itself.

Limits

Max file size256 KB
Files per transferOne. Zip several files first.
Receiving needsA camera, and a browser that exposes the camera API on HTTPS.
IntegritySHA-256 is checked before you can save. A mismatch saves nothing.
ScreenHold the phone steady, 30–50 cm away. Maximum brightness.
PaperA4 or Letter, printed black on white at 100% scale.
ExampleA 184 KB file is 111 source packets, 149 symbols and 17 printed pages (9 per page, 3 × 3).
